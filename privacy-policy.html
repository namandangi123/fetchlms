FetchLMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

FetchLMS does not collect, sell, share, or transmit any personal data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Everything the extension stores stays in your browser, on your device.

What FetchLMS does

FetchLMS adds a Fetch button to supported LMS pages. When you press it, the extension finds the document or video you have open and starts a download through your browser’s Save As dialog, using a clean file name.

What is stored on your device

FetchLMS uses your browser’s extension storage to keep:

  • Your settings, such as the file name pattern and on/off options.
  • A local list of files you have already downloaded (their web addresses and names), used only to show a duplicate warning.

This information never leaves your device. You can clear the download list from the extension’s settings page, and removing the extension deletes all of it.

What the extension reads on a page

On the supported LMS and file-hosting pages, FetchLMS reads the address of the open file and the page’s title and headings, only to name the downloaded file properly. This information is used on your device and is not sent anywhere or saved beyond the download list described above.

Network activity

FetchLMS does not send data to any server operated by its developer or by any third party. The only network request it causes is your browser downloading the file you chose from the LMS or its file host, exactly as it would if you downloaded the file manually. FetchLMS does not use any AI service and does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • downloads: to save the file you chose to download.
  • storage: to keep your settings and local download list.
  • Access to the supported LMS site and its file-hosting domain: to show the Fetch button and download the file you opened.

Children’s privacy

FetchLMS collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Independence

FetchLMS is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university or LMS provider. Please download only files you are allowed to access and keep.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that involves collecting data would also be shown in the extension’s store listing before it takes effect.
